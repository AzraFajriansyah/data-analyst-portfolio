# Data Analyst Portfolio

Welcome to my Data Analyst Portfolio! This repository showcases selected data analytics projects that demonstrate my ability to transform raw data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and data visualization.

Each project focuses on a different business context, from retail product availability and revenue opportunities to cafe sales performance and airline delays. Together, they reflect my approach to analyzing data, identifying patterns, and communicating findings that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Projects

### 1. ASOS Product Availability & Revenue Opportunity Analysis

**Focus:** Product availability, stockout analysis, and potential revenue exposure.

This project explores how product availability varies across brands in the ASOS product catalog. Using Python, I cleaned product pricing and size availability data, inferred brand names from product descriptions, and calculated stockout metrics to identify potential revenue opportunities.

**Key highlights:**

- Cleaned and standardized product data using Pandas and NumPy.
- Calculated stockout counts and rates based on unavailable sizes.
- Compared brand-level pricing, availability risks, and estimated revenue exposure.
- Visualized the relationship between product prices and stockout rates.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

### 2. Cafe Sales Performance Dashboard

**Focus:** Sales performance, product analysis, and branch-level comparisons.

This project transforms transaction data from six cafe branches across Indonesia into an Excel-based sales performance dashboard. The workbook consolidates transaction records, enriches them with product information, and presents key metrics to support business performance monitoring.

**Key highlights:**

- Consolidated and enriched sales transaction data.
- Compared revenue performance across six branches.
- Identified leading products and category-level sales contributions.
- Analyzed daily sales trends to highlight periods of increased activity.
- Built an Excel dashboard for accessible business reporting.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, Charts, Data Cleaning, Dashboard Design

### 3. US Flight Delay Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

**Focus:** Flight delay trends, airline and airport comparisons, and reported delay causes.

This project examines historical flight-delay data across the United States to identify patterns in delay volumes, seasonal trends, and airport-level concentrations. An interactive Tableau dashboard presents the findings across airlines, airports, time periods, and reported delay categories.

**Key highlights:**

- Cleaned and prepared monthly airline and airport delay data.
- Analyzed flight delay volumes and rates over time.
- Identified major airport delay hotspots and airlines with high delayed-flight counts.
- Compared reported delay causes, including carrier, weather, NAS, and late-aircraft delays.
- Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to communicate the findings.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Tableau, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization
